Where the quakes happened

What to notice

  • Light blue quakes are shallow. They trace the edges: a western arc and a long southeast arc.
  • Dark blue quakes are deep. They crowd into a band near 180°E, right under Fiji.
  • Hover any dot for its magnitude, depth and station count.

A plate, seen from the side

Drag to rotate. Light quakes spread across the top; the dark deep ones form a column rising from the floor of the plot.

Two families of earthquakes

45% of the quakes are deeper than 300 km, yet few sit in between. Most are either shallow or very deep.

Bigger quakes, more stations

Correlation r = 0.85. A stronger quake is picked up by more instruments. (Points are nudged sideways slightly so the 0.1-magnitude columns don’t overlap.)

Takeaways

  • Quakes between 178°E and 182°E (405 of them) have a median depth of 548 km. Everywhere else it is 136 km.
  • Depth is two-humped: 45% of events are deeper than 300 km, with a thin middle.
  • Magnitude tracks how many stations pick up an event (r = 0.85).
